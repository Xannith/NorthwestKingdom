Member Access Is Not Ready

Why there is no one to contact about logging in yet.

This site is still under construction. The member area exists, but it is not finished well enough for people to log in and use it without running into dead ends, empty pages, and features that look real but do not work yet.

Rather than hand out accounts and let neighbors fall into those holes, member access is on hold until the member area is genuinely usable. That means no account requests are being processed, and there is no coordinator or administrator waiting on the other end of a form.

What this means right now

  • Member logins are not being issued, so there is nothing to request or reset.
  • Pages under the member area are scaffolding. Most are placeholders.
  • The contact page is not built either, and no one monitors it.

What you can use today

Everything genuinely ready is public and needs no account: Public Documents, About NWK, the Calendar, and Community Shirts.

For Miccosukee Land Co-op information, see miccosukeelandcoop.net, which is the authoritative source for the Co-op's governing documents.
