Invasive-Plant Work Parties

NWK volunteer work on invasive plants, 2022 to 2026. Newest entry first.

NWK neighbors periodically gather to control invasive plants on neighborhood common land. Documented work includes clearing English ivy, ardisia, Nandina, smilax, and climbing grape vines around the delta/Y in November 2023, followed by wildflower planting in the cleared area. In February 2025, neighbors continued invasive removal and berry collection along NWK Way. Additional planning and treatment has focused on coral ardisia and Nandina near the south-side road split. The effort has been supported at different times by Jerrie Lindsey, Karen Rose, Denise Tinley, Kim Reimer, Josh Bechtel, Betsi Heinlen, and other neighbors.
How to read this page. Entries are tagged by how strongly the record supports them. Completed means a post-event message reports the work or thanks named participants for work done that day. Scheduled means the date, time, location, and intended work are corroborated, but no completion message was found. Planning or contracted means related invasive work that was not a neighborhood volunteer work party. Being copied on an email does not establish attendance, so participant lists name only people the correspondence directly supports.

NWK work parties

February 7, 2026, 2:00 p.m. — coral ardisia and Nandina at the split

Scheduled South side of NWK, just before the road splits — described as “at the split.”

Planned first-stage attack on coral ardisia and Nandina: cut and collect all berries, then return on another day to cut or dig out the plants.

Jerrie Lindsey proposed and organized the work. Denise Tinley, Josh Bechtel, and Betsi Heinlen confirmed the plan. No post-event message documenting completion has been found, so this should be described as a planned work party rather than a completed accomplishment until photographs, notes, or participant confirmation turn up.

February 8, 2025, 10:00 a.m. — NWK Way invasive removal

Completed Along NWK Way; exact segment not stated.

Participants continued eliminating invasive plants, cutting or digging plants and collecting berries to reduce reseeding. The preparation email asked volunteers to bring gloves, loppers, shovels, and containers for collecting berries. Denise Tinley's same-day report said, “We made so much progress today!”

Participants supported by the record: Denise Tinley, who reported the work and explicitly thanked Jerrie Lindsey, Josh Bechtel, and Kim Reimer.

November 5, 2023 — wildflower planting at the delta/Y

Completed Follow-up on the cleared delta/Y patch.

Remaining ivy was to be pulled and wildflower seed was planted in the cleared area. Denise Tinley shared photographs and described it as a beautiful afternoon planting the seeds.

Participants supported by the record: Denise Tinley and Jerrie Lindsey are directly connected to the follow-up correspondence. A complete attendance list is not stated.

November 4, 2023 — delta/Y restoration

Completed Around the delta/Y in NWK.

The group cleared a sunny patch, removing English ivy, ardisia, Nandina, smilax, and grape vines that were climbing into the tree canopy. Jerrie Lindsey reported the result and shared before-and-after photographs.

Participants supported by the record: Jerrie Lindsey reported and photographed the completed work, Josh Bechtel organized the work-party plan, and Denise Tinley confirmed the location beforehand. Karen Rose, Kim Reimer, and Roque Ramirez were included in the correspondence, but copying alone does not establish attendance.

April 17, 2023 — invasive work along NWK Way

Planning or contracted Common land along NWK Way, near the grassy field where the brick chimney had been.

Karen Rose reported that Kristan would spend multiple hours working on invasives. Karen Rose coordinated and Kristan performed the planned work. This appears to have been individual or contracted work rather than a volunteer work party.

March 25, 2023, morning — workday scheduled, likely redirected

Scheduled NWK common land; firebreak areas included the road edges, the route from Jerrie Lindsey's house toward Any Old Way, and the area around her outbuilding.

Karen Rose had an NWK invasive-plant workday scheduled and proposed using the time for raking and other preparation for the planned prescribed burn if that work was unfinished. Josh Bechtel and Jerrie Lindsey were coordinating burn preparation. No email confirming invasive removal that day was found, so this should not be counted as confirmed invasive work.

February 10, 2023 — grant-area planning and field meeting

Planning or contracted Wooded land behind Josh Bechtel's house and land bordering NWK Way.

Karen Rose and Josh Bechtel arranged an afternoon field meeting after Karen Rose described a grant covering invasive removal in these areas. No completion record was found.
